Delete your LifeOS account

Deleting your LifeOS account permanently removes, immediately: your account (email and password proof), the encrypted copy of your data on the sync service, your synced photos, and every device's sign-in. This cannot be undone.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open LifeOS and go to Settings.
  2. In the LifeOS account card at the top, choose Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Here (instant)

Enter the email and password of the account to delete.

By email (if you can't sign in)

Email pokemonboy0505@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my LifeOS account". We'll confirm it's you and delete the account and its synced data within 7 days.

What's deleted, and what isn't

  • Deleted: your account, the encrypted synced copy of your records and photos, and all device sign-ins.
  • Not touched: data stored on your own devices. Remove it with Settings → Erase, or by uninstalling the app.
  • AI usage counts (no content) expire within 2 days; reports you sent about AI answers are deleted after 180 days.
  • Google Play keeps its own record of purchases. Cancel any subscription in Google Play first — deleting the account does not cancel billing.
