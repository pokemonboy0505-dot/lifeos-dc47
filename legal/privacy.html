LifeOS Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

LifeOS is a planner, money tracker, study helper and wellbeing app. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your records live on your device. If you create a LifeOS account, the copy that syncs between your devices is encrypted on your device first, so we cannot read it. We never sell data and never show ads.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your records: tasks, goals, habits, money entries and budgets, savings goals, study materials (including photos of notes you add), flashcards and test results, study sessions, screen-time entries you record, AI conversations, and settings.
  • Profile: the name you choose, and optionally an email address and an app password.

This data stays on the device unless you turn on one of the features below.

LifeOS account (optional sync)

If you create a LifeOS account, a copy of your records and photos is stored on our sync service (Cloudflare) so your other devices can get it. Before it leaves your device it is encrypted with a key made from your password. The service only ever holds:

  • your email address;
  • a one-way proof of your password (it cannot be turned back into your password);
  • the encrypted copy of your data, which we cannot open;
  • sign-in records for your devices (when they were last used, and the device type).

Because only your password unlocks the data, a forgotten password cannot be reset by us.

AI features

AI features (the AI coach, StudyMate lessons, practice tests, flashcards, tutoring, reading photos of notes, report summaries) send what is needed for that request to the LifeOS AI service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers AI using models hosted by Cloudflare. For example, the notes or photos you ask StudyMate to work from, or — for the coach — totals and counts from the modules you have switched on (never individual transactions or private notes). You control the coach's access module by module.

  • The AI service does not keep the content of your requests. It keeps a running count of AI usage per day and per device (a random device ID made by the app), to enforce fair-use limits.
  • If you report an AI answer, the answer, the reason you chose and any note you write are kept for up to 180 days so we can review it.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Do not rely on them for financial, medical or other important decisions.

Purchases

Premium and AI credits are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive a confirmation of what was bought, never your card or payment details. What you bought is recorded in your LifeOS data so it can follow your account to your other devices.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of data, ever.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No reading of other apps, messages, contacts or location.

Deleting your data

  • Account: in the app, Settings → LifeOS account → Delete account. This removes your account and the encrypted copy from the sync service immediately. You can also delete it on the account deletion page.
  • This device: Settings → Erase, or uninstall the app.

Children

LifeOS is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please use LifeOS only with a parent or guardian, who should create and manage any account and approve any purchase.

Security

Synced data is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256), sent over HTTPS, and stored encrypted. Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security; you can also set an app password in Settings.
